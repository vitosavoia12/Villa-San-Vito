A quieter way to experience the Amalfi Coast

There are places you visit. And there are places that feel like a secret discovered too late.

High above Raito, a small village suspended over the Amalfi Coast, there is a villa where time seems to move more slowly.

Mornings begin with the scent of lemon trees, windows open to the endless blue of the Gulf of Salerno, and every terrace becomes one of Italy’s most beautiful balconies.

Villa San Vito is not just a place to stay. It is a way to experience the Amalfi Coast like a local: away from the tourist noise, yet close to everything that makes this corner of Italy unforgettable.
