Raito, sunlit memory

A village suspended between mountain, sea and time

The name Raito carries an old suggestion of sunlight. Set above Vietri sul Mare, the village opens toward the Gulf of Salerno and sits between rock, lemon trees and the quiet blue of the coast.

Villa San Vito belongs to that landscape. An old nineteenth-century home, it preserves the atmosphere of local architecture: stone, terraces, shade, sea air and rooms made to stay cool through long Mediterranean days.

Its story is not separate from the place around it. To arrive here is to step into Raito's slower rhythm, where the coast is seen from above and the evening light turns the villa into a small balcony over the Gulf.

From Villa San Vito, you can spend the day discovering some of the most beautiful places in the area.

Vietri sul Mare

A nearby coastal town known for ceramics, sea views and relaxed local character.
